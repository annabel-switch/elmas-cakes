Frequently Asked Questions 💡

How far in advance should I place my order?

For custom celebration cakes, we recommend ordering 1–2 days ahead. Simple cakes and cupcake boxes can often be fulfilled same-day or within 24 hours!

Do you deliver outside Benin City?

We primarily offer doorstep delivery across Benin City. For special orders outside Benin City, please send us a direct WhatsApp message!

Can I request custom flavors or designs not on the menu?

Yes! Simply reach out to us via the floating WhatsApp button with your inspiration picture or custom requests.
